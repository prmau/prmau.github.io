Findings: Methodological insights and technological adaptions that expand how par- ticipation can occur in distributed and digitally mediated collaborative environments

Fostering an Inclusive Community Among Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE) Students with Mixed-Reality Technologies at a Hispanic-Serving Institution (HSI)

ASEE CoNECD 2025 · with Redwan Ul Haque Choyon, Theodore Platt, Bill Hamilton, Hilda Cecilia Contreras Aguirre, Luis Rodolfo Garcia Carrillo

Question: How can mixed-reality technologies foster an inclusive community among engineering students at a Hispanic-Serving Institution?

Approach: 9 participants in person participatory design sessions

Findings: This study has revealed how even subtle representational choices in digital environments shape the interpersonal and ethical dimensions of participation.

Read the paper

Utilization of advanced mathematical methods to analyze correlations in-vehicle measurement data

Question: Anlyzing the possible engine parameters having a maximum influence on undesired engine behavior like fuel consumption and emission by finding the correlations between real-time engine parameters and emission/fuel consumption data.

Approach: Application of clustering algorithms in finding correlation of engine parameters

Outcomes: A software that could read mult-dimensional parameters data from the excel sheet and display the correlation with accuracy
